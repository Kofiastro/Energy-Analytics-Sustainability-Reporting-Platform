# Energy Analytics Executive Report

## Overview

An analysis was conducted on 409 building energy consumption records from the ASHRAE Energy Dataset to identify consumption patterns, efficiency opportunities, and sustainability metrics.

## Key Findings

### Total Energy Consumption

The portfolio of buildings consumed 147,395.90 energy units during the reporting period.

### Carbon Emissions

Estimated carbon emissions totaled 63,380.24 kg CO₂ based on the applied emissions factor.

### Building Type Analysis

Education facilities accounted for the highest energy consumption, representing approximately 43.6% of total portfolio energy usage.

### Seasonal Trends

Energy consumption peaked in August with 26,447.77 units, indicating a period of elevated energy demand.

### Temperature Impact

A weak negative correlation (-0.111) was observed between air temperature and energy consumption, suggesting that weather conditions were not a primary driver of energy use within the analyzed dataset.

### Building Size Impact

A moderate positive correlation (0.488) was identified between building size and energy consumption, indicating that larger facilities generally consume more energy.

### Energy Intensity Analysis

Building 7 exhibited the highest energy intensity, making it a priority candidate for energy efficiency assessments and optimization initiatives.

## Recommendations

1. Prioritize high-intensity buildings for detailed energy audits.
2. Investigate operational factors contributing to elevated August consumption.
3. Develop targeted efficiency strategies for educational facilities.
4. Implement ongoing KPI monitoring through Power BI dashboards.
5. Establish carbon reduction targets and sustainability tracking metrics.
